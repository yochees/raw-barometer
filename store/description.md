# Store listing

## Name
Raw Barometer

## Description
Do you want to see the actual barometer reading without switching between the Barometer and Altimeter modes?

Here is the solution. Raw Barometer shows the raw (station) pressure in hPa: the value the sensor measures, not reduced to sea level and not corrected for altitude. The app has a glance and a full view, with the same familiar look as the Garmin native Barometer.

- Glance: current pressure and the trend line.
- Full view: current pressure and a graph with the minimum and maximum readings.
- Push START, DOWN or UP to change the graph period: 4, 12 or 24 hours.

Made for the fenix 8 MIP (Solar 47/51 mm, 43/47 mm, Pro 47 mm).
