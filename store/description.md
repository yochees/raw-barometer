# Store listing

## Name
Raw Barometer

## Description
Do you want to see the actual barometer reading without switching between the Barometer and Altimeter modes?

Here is the solution. Raw Barometer shows the raw (station) pressure: the value the sensor measures, not reduced to sea level and not corrected for altitude. The app has a glance and a full view, with the same familiar look as the Garmin native Barometer.

Why not set the watch to Barometer mode? In Barometer mode the watch thinks that your elevation does not change. This has two problems:
- In an airplane, it still does not show the correct pressure.
- During a workout, it can show zero elevation change.

Raw Barometer shows the sensor value in all modes, so you can keep the Auto mode.

- Glance: current pressure and the trend line.
- Full view: current pressure and a graph with the minimum and maximum readings.
- Push START, DOWN or UP to change the graph period: 4, 12 or 24 hours.
- Unit: hPa, mbar, inHg or mmHg. Set it in the Connect IQ phone app, or on the watch: in the full view, hold UP (MENU).

Compatible watches (280x280 memory-in-pixel screen):
- fenix 8 Solar 51mm, tactix 8 Solar 51mm
- fenix 9 Pro Solar 51mm
- fenix 7X, fenix 7X Pro, fenix 7X Pro Solar (no Wi-Fi), tactix 7
- Enduro 2, Enduro 3, Enduro 4
